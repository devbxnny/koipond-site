# 🐟 Koi Pond Guide

Welcome to the pond!

**Koi Pond** is a community Discord game where everyone in your server cares for the same shared koi pond.

Feed the koi, earn Pond Coins, collect fish, play minigames, buy upgrades, activate server boosts, climb the leaderboards, and grow your pond together. ♡

## 🎛️ The Pond Panel

Most of Koi Pond is played directly through the buttons underneath your server's pond.

### 🍚 Feed Koi

Feed the pond when the koi are ready.

A successful feeding:

- Earns Pond Coins.
- Adds to your personal feeding count.
- Helps your server's pond progress.
- Can result in a collectible koi encounter.
- Can activate bonuses from items and pond boosts.

You **cannot feed twice in a row**.

If you were the last person to feed the pond, another pond keeper must take the next turn.

The pond also has a feeding cooldown. The panel tells you when the koi will be ready again.

---

### 🎮 Play

Open the Koi Pond minigame menu.

Current games include:

- 🃏 **Koi Memory**
- 🐟 **Go-Fish**
- 🐸 **Lily Pad Hop**

Choose a game and follow the prompts inside Discord.

---

### 🛒 Shop

Spend the Pond Coins you earn from playing and feeding.

Some purchases are personal items while others activate bonuses for the entire server.

See the full shop section below for details.

---

### 🐟 Koi

Open your personal koi collection for that server.

The collection browser lets you:

- Browse your koi.
- View caught and uncaught koi.
- Filter your collection.
- Sort your collection.
- Browse by rarity.
- Inspect koi you have caught.
- View koi artwork and catch information.
- Mark a koi as your favorite.

Your collection is personal to you **within that server**.

---

### 📊 Stats

View your personal pond statistics for the current server.

Your stats include:

- 🪙 Pond Coins
- 🍚 Total feedings

---

### 🏆 Leaderboard

Open the leaderboard menu.

There are two kinds of leaderboards:

**Players**

See the top pond keepers in your current server, including:

- Richest players by Pond Coins.
- Most active players by total feedings.

**Ponds**

See the global pond leaderboard and compare your server's pond with other Koi Pond servers.

Global ponds are ranked by total pond feedings.

---

### 🥣 Communal Bowl

Work together with other players to fill a shared bowl using Pond Coins.

More on this below!

Refresh the pond panel to display the latest pond state, progress, cooldown, and active boosts.

# 🐟 Collecting Koi

Successful feedings can lead to collectible koi encounters.

Koi are divided into different rarities:

- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- Mythic
- Limited
- Secret

When you catch a koi for the first time, it is added to your personal collection for that server.

If you catch a koi you already own, it counts as a **duplicate catch** and awards bonus Pond Coins instead.

Some koi are not available through normal feeding.

Limited, seasonal, event, and special koi may have their own requirements.

---

## ⭐ Favorite Koi

You can choose one of your caught koi as your favorite.

Your favorite koi may appear alongside you in pond interactions, giving your pond identity a little extra fih flavor.

# 🪙 Pond Coins

Pond Coins are Koi Pond's main in-game currency.

You earn coins through successful feedings and certain bonuses.

Coins can be spent on:

- Personal feeding items.
- Permanent upgrades.
- Temporary server buffs.
- Communal Bowl contributions.
- Extremely questionable bread purchases.

Coins and items belong to your player profile in the server where you earned them.

---

# 🛒 Pond Shop

Open **Shop** from the pond panel.

## 🍚 Premium Koi Food — 75 Coins

Your next successful feeding counts as **2 feedings worth of pond progress**.

The item waits in your inventory until it is used on a successful feeding.

---

## 🍵 Tea Party — 100 Coins

Activates a **30-minute server-wide boost**.

While Tea Party is active, successful feedings earn:

**+5 bonus Pond Coins**

---

## ⏱️ Lucky Bell — 150 Coins

Immediately removes **25% of the pond's current remaining feeding cooldown**.

If the pond is already ready to eat, the Lucky Bell is not used and you are not charged.

---

## 🌸 Lotus Blessing — 200 Coins

Activates a **30-minute server-wide boost**.

While active, each successful feeding has a chance to **double the coins earned from that feeding**.

---

## 🎏 Koi Treat Bag — 250 Coins

Contains:

**3 Premium Koi Food charges**

Buying the bag adds all three charges to your inventory.

---

## 🐸 Frog Friend — 300 Coins

A permanent personal upgrade.

Once your Frog Friend joins you, successful feedings earn:

**+5 bonus Pond Coins**

You only need one Frog Friend.

He is small. He is employed.

---

## 🎂 Pond Party Cake — 500 Coins

Activates a powerful **30-minute server-wide pond boost**.

While active:

- Feeding cooldowns are reduced by 50%.
- Successful feedings earn +5 bonus Pond Coins.
- Successful feedings give +1 extra pond progress.

Buying another Pond Party Cake extends the duration.

---

## 🍀 Lucky Feed — 200 Coins

Stored in your inventory until your next successful feeding.

That feeding:

- Guarantees a koi encounter.
- Uses boosted rarity odds.

Lucky Feed's boosted rarity pool includes:

- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- Mythic

---

## 📦 Mystery Pond Parcel — 150 Coins

Open a suspicious parcel immediately.

Possible outcomes include:

- Premium Koi Food
- 3 Premium Koi Food
- Golden Pellet
- Lucky Feed
- Complete pond garbage

Sometimes gambling on a soggy package is character development.

---

## 🟡 Golden Pellet — 125 Coins

Stored until your next successful feeding.

That feeding earns **triple the normal base feeding coins**.

---

## 🥖 Illegal Bread — 25 Coins

You were explicitly told not to feed the koi bread.

This item provides no sensible financial benefit.

The pond remembers.

# 🥣 Communal Bowl

The Communal Bowl lets the entire server work together toward a powerful pond-wide event.

The bowl requires:

**1,000 Pond Coins**

Each player can contribute up to:

**200 Pond Coins per bowl**

Available donation buttons include:

- 25 Coins
- 50 Coins
- 100 Coins
- 200 Coins

When the bowl reaches 1,000 coins, a **60-minute Feeding Frenzy** begins.

Contributions reset when the frenzy begins.

Donations are paused while Feeding Frenzy is already active.

---

# 🌧️ Feeding Frenzy

Feeding Frenzy lasts for **60 minutes**.

While active:

- The pond's feeding cooldown is cut in half.
- Successful feedings earn **+5 bonus Pond Coins**.

The pond panel displays Feeding Frenzy while it is active.

# 🌸 Naming Your Pond

Server members with **Manage Server** permission can use:

`/pondname`

to give the server's pond a custom name.

Changing the pond name updates the server's registered pond panels.

---

# 🔔 Feed-Ready Notifications

Server managers can configure automatic notifications using:

`/pondsetup`

Notifications can alert your community when the koi are hungry and ready to be fed again.

Inside the setup menu, admins can:

- Choose a notification role.
- Choose a notification channel.
- Enable or disable notifications.
- Send a test notification.
- Change the pond's theme.

You must have **Manage Server** permission to change these settings.

The bot only pings the configured notification role.

# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

## Why can't I feed?

There are two common reasons:

**The koi are still full.**  
Wait until the feeding cooldown ends.

**You were the last person to feed.**  
Someone else must take the next turn.

---

## Are my coins shared with everyone?

No.

Your Pond Coins are personal to you within that server.

The **Communal Bowl** is shared, but players choose when to donate their personal coins to it.

---

## Are my koi shared with the server?

No.

Each player has their own koi collection within each server.

---

## Why did I catch a koi I already own?

Duplicate catches can happen.

Instead of adding another copy to your collection, duplicate catches award bonus Pond Coins.

---

## Does the pond belong to one channel?

The pond belongs to the **server**.

Use `/pond` to post a pond panel where your community wants to interact with it.

---

## Where can I see when the pond is ready?

Check the main pond panel.

It displays when the pond will be ready to feed again.

You can also ask your server admins to enable feed-ready notifications.
