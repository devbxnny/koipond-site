# 🐟 Koi Pond Guide

Welcome to the pond!

Koi Pond is a community Discord bot where everyone in your server works together to care for a shared koi pond.

Feed the koi, earn Pond Coins, collect fish, unlock upgrades, and grow your pond together. ♡

---

## 🌊 Getting Started

1. Add Koi Pond to your Discord server.

2. Choose the channel where you want your pond to live.

3. Use `/pondsetup` to create your community pond. You need the **Manage Server** permission.

4. Start feeding the koi!

You can use `/pondguide` inside Discord whenever you need a reminder.

## 🍚 Feeding the Koi

Press **Feed Koi** whenever the pond is hungry.

Every successful feeding:

- Earns Pond Coins.
- Contributes to your server's pond progress.
- Increases your personal feeding count.
- Gives you a chance to catch a collectible koi.

You cannot feed the pond twice in a row. Another pond keeper must take the next turn!

As your pond levels up, the koi stay full longer between normal feedings.

## 🐟 Collecting Koi

Every successful feeding has a **50% chance** to catch a koi.

Koi come in different rarities, and every fish you catch belongs to your personal collection.

Press **🐟 Koi** on the pond panel to see your collection.

From there, you can:

- Inspect individual koi and their artwork.
- See when you caught each fish.
- Choose your favorite koi.
- Show off your catches to the server.

You can catch duplicates, too!

### Current catch rates

These are the chances per successful feeding for the current collection pool:

| Result | Chance |
|---|---:|
| Common koi | 27.78% |
| Uncommon koi | 13.89% |
| Rare koi | 8.33% |

Future rarities will change the distribution as more koi are released.

## 🪙 Pond Coins and the Shop

Earn Pond Coins by feeding the koi.

Open **Shop** on the pond panel to spend your coins on food, upgrades, and temporary boosts.

Some items help you personally, while others benefit everyone in your server.

## 🥣 Communal Bowl

Everyone can contribute Pond Coins to the Communal Bowl.

Fill the bowl to activate a **60-minute Feeding Frenzy** for your server!

During Feeding Frenzy, the pond's feeding cooldown is reduced and successful feedings earn bonus coins.

## ✨ Boosts

Certain shop items provide temporary bonuses, including extra coins or shorter feeding waits.

Check the pond panel to see which boosts are currently active.

## 🏆 Leaderboards

Open **Leaderboard** to see your server's top pond keepers or compare your pond's progress with other servers.

## 🌸 Limited Koi

Some koi cannot be caught through normal feeding.

Limited koi may be awarded through special events or other pond celebrations.

**Beta Sakura** is a special Limited koi for beta testers.
