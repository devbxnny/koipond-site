# Koi Pond Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Koi Pond Discord application ("Koi Pond," "the bot," or "the service") collects, stores, and uses when you interact with the service.

Koi Pond is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Discord Inc.

## 1. Information Koi Pond Collects

Koi Pond collects and stores limited information necessary to operate its features.

This may include:

### Discord Information

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server (guild) IDs
- Discord channel IDs when required for server-specific features
- Usernames or display names when needed to display Koi Pond features

### Gameplay Information

Koi Pond may store gameplay information associated with your Discord account or server, including:

- Virtual currency balances
- Feeding activity and statistics
- Feeding cooldowns
- Koi collections and collectible ownership
- Virtual items and upgrades
- Communal Bowl contributions
- Pond progress and levels
- Pond names and server-specific settings
- Leaderboard statistics
- Beta participation and reward claims
- Other gameplay progress necessary to operate Koi Pond

## 2. How Information Is Used

Koi Pond uses this information only as necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the service.

This includes:

- Saving gameplay progress
- Associating game data with the correct Discord user or server
- Managing virtual currency and collections
- Operating server-specific ponds
- Enforcing gameplay cooldowns and limits
- Providing statistics and leaderboards
- Preventing duplicate reward claims
- Operating events and communal features
- Diagnosing technical problems
- Preventing abuse or exploitation
- Maintaining and improving Koi Pond

## 3. Discord Message Content

Koi Pond primarily operates through Discord application commands, buttons, and other Discord interactions.

Koi Pond does not use Discord message content for advertising or user profiling.

If Koi Pond's use of message content materially changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 4. Sharing of Information

Koi Pond does not sell or rent personal information to advertisers.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate Koi Pond, such as Discord and infrastructure or hosting providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect Koi Pond, its users, or its systems.

Some gameplay information may be visible to other Discord users through Koi Pond features. For example, usernames, pond statistics, leaderboard rankings, collections, or other game-related information may be displayed as part of the game.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Koi Pond gameplay data is stored on infrastructure used to operate the service.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

Koi Pond retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service and preserve gameplay progress.

Information may remain stored until it is no longer necessary, the relevant data is deleted, or Koi Pond is discontinued.

Backups may temporarily contain previously deleted information until those backups are replaced or expire.

## 7. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of data associated with your Discord account by contacting the Koi Pond developer through the official Koi Pond community/support server.

Server owners or administrators may also contact the developer regarding data associated with their Discord server.

Some minimal information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or enforcement of the Koi Pond Terms of Service.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Koi Pond is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements.

Koi Pond does not knowingly collect information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Koi Pond operates through Discord and relies on Discord's services to function.

Your use of Discord is separately governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policies.

Koi Pond may also use hosting or infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Koi Pond develops or its data practices change.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will show when the policy was most recently revised.

## 11. Contact

Questions, concerns, or requests regarding your information can be submitted through the official Koi Pond community/support server.
