# 🐟 Koi Pond

Welcome to the official website for **Koi Pond** — a community Discord game where your server works together to care for a shared koi pond.

Feed the koi, earn Pond Coins, collect rare fish, play minigames, activate boosts, customize your pond, and climb the leaderboards together. ♡

## 🌊 Your Server, Your Pond

Every Discord server gets its own shared pond.

Your community can:

- 🍚 Feed the koi and grow the pond.
- 🪙 Earn and spend Pond Coins.
- 🐟 Build personal koi collections.
- 🎮 Play Koi Pond minigames.
- 🥣 Fill the Communal Bowl together.
- ✨ Activate powerful pond-wide boosts.
- 🏆 Compete on player and global pond leaderboards.
- 🎨 Customize the pond with names and themes.

The more your community plays, the more your pond grows.

## 🐟 Collect Koi

Every feeding could lead to a new koi encounter.

Collect fish across multiple rarities, including:

## 🎮 Play Together

Koi Pond isn't just about feeding fish.

Open the **Play** menu to jump into Discord minigames like:

- 🃏 **Koi Memory**
- 🐟 **Go-Fish**
- 🐸 **Lily Pad Hop**

Earn rewards, challenge yourself, or drag your friends into fish-related nonsense.

---

## 🛒 Spend Your Pond Coins

The Pond Shop contains personal upgrades, useful feeding items, and server-wide boosts.

You can:

- Improve pond progress.
- Earn bonus coins.
- Reduce feeding cooldowns.
- Guarantee koi encounters.
- Trigger server-wide bonuses.
- Buy a tiny frog.
- Purchase bread despite being specifically told not to.

Financial responsibility is not guaranteed.

---

## 🥣 Work Together

The **Communal Bowl** lets everyone contribute Pond Coins toward a shared goal.

Fill the bowl to trigger a **Feeding Frenzy**, giving the whole server faster feeding and bonus rewards for a limited time.

Koi Pond is built around community progression — one person can help, but the pond grows fastest when everyone gets involved.

Track the most dedicated pond keepers in your server or compare your pond against other communities.
